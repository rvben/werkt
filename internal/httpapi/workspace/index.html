Operate from the keyboard

A compact operator guide for reading health, diagnosing failures, and intervening without losing revision truth.

Connect and read health

The connection status is live. “Needs attention” means the authoritative latest run failed; paused automations remain visible and manual diagnostic runs stay available.

Intervene safely

Pause stops new ingress while queued and running work continues. Resume starts from the next future schedule. Rollback changes the active revision, while running jobs keep their pinned revision.

Diagnose and recover

Start with the exact failure, then inspect logs or copy the diagnostic bundle. A diagnostic rerun shows and atomically pins its target revision before work is queued.

Verify the outcome

Receipts persist until dismissed. Use View audit to verify attributed lifecycle changes; history views load 100 records at a time and offer older pages when available.
